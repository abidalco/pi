    The Karaoke Will

    A broke younger brother inherits nothing but his father's karaoke machine and his debts, until the machine turns out to hold proof that Dad wrote a famous hit. The only way to get paid is to sue alongside the older brother he left behind.

    Synopsis, tone, themes

    Cornered by a debt collector, he gets the call: Dad is dead, the will is ready. The vault holds one thing, the karaoke machine. It goes to court as a custody hearing. Track 7 proves authorship; the brothers need both halves of the evidence, sue together, and the royalties clear the debt.

    Tone: bittersweet · loud · Themes: inheritance is debt · you become your father

    Logic: the machine is the evidence🤖 · 🔥1Track 7 predates the hit's release by two years. Selling the machine kills the claim.
